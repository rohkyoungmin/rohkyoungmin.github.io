Summary

I am an undergraduate cybersecurity researcher at Dankook University, working at the intersection of machine learning and systems security. I have developed methods for concept-drift-resilient Android malware detection and a lightweight ML-KEM architecture for Arm TrustZone-A. As first author, I have published an article in an SCIE Q1 journal and an ACM SAC 2026 paper recognized with the BK21+ Outstanding Conference distinction, and my work has received paper awards at KCC and KSC. I focus on turning security research into practical systems and validating it through quantitative evaluation.
